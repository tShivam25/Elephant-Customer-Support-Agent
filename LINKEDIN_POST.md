🚀 Just wrapped up our submission for the Vectorize Hackathon! 

Meet **ResolveMind** 🧠 - a customer support AI that actually REMEMBERS you. 

We were tired of "goldfish AI" that makes you repeat your problems and suggests the same failed fixes over and over. By integrating **Hindsight by Vectorize**, we built an agent that maintains a long-term memory bank for every single customer.

🛠️ **How it works under the hood:**
1️⃣ Before answering, it recalls your past tickets, preferences, and failed fixes.
2️⃣ It adapts its tool usage—if it knows "clearing the cache" failed last week, it escalates immediately.
3️⃣ After the chat, it automatically extracts and retains structured facts for the future.

No more repeating yourself. Just context-aware, intelligent support.
